Try this

1. Arm with Y, start with Space, and play pads after the count-in. Notes land on 1/16 (TC).

2. Press U to undo the take. Press it again to redo.

3. Open GRID (Shift, then pad 3). Pads are the 16 steps of the selected row.

4. Open TRIM (Shift, then pad 7), select pad 15 (BREAK) and press F5 to CHOP it to bank B.

5. Drop a WAV or MP3 on any pad to load it.

Sounds and effects here are browser stand-ins. On the unit, the stock engine plays the pads and runs the effects.
